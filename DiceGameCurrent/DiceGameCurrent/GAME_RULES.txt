1. BASEBALL DICE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GAME OVERVIEW
  * Players: 2. Player 1 pitches first; Player 2 bats first (Home Team advantage).
  * Length: Selected before match start (3, 6, or 9 innings). Three outs per half-inning.
  * Objective: Score the most runs by matching pitch and swing tactics.

TURN STRUCTURE
  1. Pitcher Style Selection: The pitcher secretly selects a Pitching Style for 
     the entire half-inning, confirming their choice to end the step.
  2. At-Bat Execution: For each at-bat, the batter chooses a Swing without seeing 
     the pitch. The pitch is drawn automatically based on the pitcher’s 
     style, and both dice are rolled simultaneously.
  3. Inning Handover: Upon the third out, the half-inning ends. Roles swap, 
     and the new pitcher selects their Pitching Style.

PITCHING STYLES & SWING MATCHUPS
  Pitching Styles:
    * Power Pitcher: Heavy emphasis on Fastballs; secondary Changeup and Slider.
    * Crafty: Heavy emphasis on Changeups; secondary Fastball and Slider.
    * Breaking Ball: Heavy emphasis on Sliders; secondary Fastball and Changeup.

  Strategic Cycle:
    * Fastball beats Power Swing | Loses to Patient Swing
    * Changeup beats Patient Swing | Loses to Contact Swing
    * Slider beats Contact Swing | Loses to Power Swing

DICE & MATCHUP MECHANICS
  Matchup Type                        Batter Dice               Pitcher Dice
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Favoured (Swing Beats Pitch)        2x Swing Die (Keep High)  1d4
  Even (Neutral Matchup)              1x Swing Die              1d8
  Unfavoured: Fastball vs. Power      1d12                      2d12 (Keep High)
  Unfavoured: Changeup vs. Patient    1d8                       1d10
  Unfavoured: Slider vs. Contact      1d10                      1d12

  * Base Swing Dice Sizes: Patient (1d8), Contact (1d10), Power (1d12).

SCORING MATRIX (Batter Roll Minus Pitcher Roll)
  Differential    Result        Effect
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  -5 or lower     Strikeout     1 Out
  -4 to +1        Field Out     1 Out (Triggers Double Play if Pitcher roll is 
                                even with a runner on 1st)
  +2              Walk          Batter to 1st; forced runners advance 1 base
  +3 to +4        Single        Batter to 1st; all base runners advance 1 base
  +5              Double        Batter to 2nd; all base runners advance 2 bases
  +6              Triple        Batter to 3rd; all base runners score
  +7 to +9        Deep Out      1 Out; runner on 3rd scores (Unless 3rd out)
  +10 or higher   Home Run      Batter and all base runners score

SPECIAL RULES
  * Favoured Immunity: A favoured swing cannot strike out (minimum net value is -3).
  * Double Maximum: Rolling the maximum value on both dice during a favoured swing 
    results in an automatic Home Run regardless of differential.
  * Third-Out Catch: A Deep Out recorded as the third out immediately ends 
    the half-inning; no run scores.

2. ROLLS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GAME OVERVIEW
  * Players: 2 or more (group play compatible).
  * Dice Pool: 15 total (12x d6, 1x d8, 1x d10, 1x d12).
  * Objective: Bank all 15 dice; lowest cumulative score wins.

PLAYER TURN
  1. Roll all remaining active dice in the pool.
  2. Select and bank at least one die.
  3. Re-roll all unbanked dice. Repeat until all 15 dice are banked to 
     finish the turn.

SCORING STRUCTURE
  A banked die scores based on its differential from its highest face value. 
  Lower scores are better.

  Die Type    Highest Face (0 Pts)    Penalty for Rolling a 1
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  d6          6                       6 Points
  d8          8                       8 Points
  d10         10                      10 Points
  d12         12                      12 Points

  * Formula: Score = Max Value - Rolled Face
  * Exception: Rolling a 1 scores penalty points equal to the die's total sides.

3. JACKPOT
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GAME OVERVIEW
  * Players: 2 (alternating turns across 5 rounds).
  * Dice Pool: 1x d4, 1x d6, 1x d8, 1x d12, 1x d20.
  * Objective: Accumulate highest total points or trigger instant-win Jackpot.

READING THE ROLL
  Each roll yields seven numbers:
    * The 3 exposed side faces of the d4.
    * The top face of the d6, d8, d12, and d20.

SCORING & INSTANT WIN CONDITIONS
  * Sets: Any number appearing two or more times among the 7 exposed values scores:
      Score = Number Value x Occurrences
  * Booster: A d20 showing 13 or higher adds its raw face value to turn score.
  * Jackpot (Instant Win): Achieved when there are ZERO sets across all 7 numbers 
    AND the d20 shows 12 or lower.

MATCH RESOLUTION
  * An uncancelled Jackpot wins the match instantly.
  * If a player rolls a Jackpot, all other players in the round complete their final 
    turn. If multiple players hit a Jackpot in the same round, they cancel 
    out and play continues.
  * If no Jackpot is hit after 5 rounds, the highest total score wins.

4. HOG
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GAME OVERVIEW
  * Players: 2 or more (alternating turns).
  * Dice Pool: 1x d6.
  * Objective: First player to bank 75 points wins instantly.

TURN MECHANICS
  1. Roll the die.
  2. Roll 2-6: Add value to running turn total. Choose to roll again or hold.
  3. Roll 1 (The Hog): Turn ends immediately and all points accumulated during 
     that turn are lost. Banked score is unaffected.
  4. Hold: Bank running turn total into overall score and end turn.

5. GENERALS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GAME OVERVIEW
  * Players: 2.
  * Dice Pool: 5x d6.
  * Structure: 12 total turns (6 per player) using a single shared scorecard 
    in a snake turn order (P1, P2, P2, P1...).

TURN STRUCTURE
  1. Roll all 5 dice.
  2. Optionally keep any number of dice and re-roll the rest. Max 3 rolls per turn.
  3. Claim one available category on the shared scorecard to end turn.

CATEGORIES & SCORING
  Category          Qualification Requirement           Score
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Ones to Sixes     Any dice showing selected face (1-6) Sum of matching dice
  Set of 3          Three dice of same face             Sum of all 5 dice
  Set of 4          Four dice of same face              Sum of all 5 dice
  Packed House      Three of one face + pair of another 25 Points
  Small Joker       Four sequential faces (1-2-3-4 etc) 30 Points
  Large Joker       Five sequential faces (1-2-3-4-5)   40 Points
  General           All five dice showing same face     50 Points

ZEROING
  If a hand does not qualify for an open category (or by choice), a player 
  must select an open category to claim for 0 points, permanently removing it 
  for both players.

6. THREE THOUSAND
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GAME OVERVIEW
  * Players: 2 or more (alternating turns).
  * Dice Pool: 6x d6.
  * Objective: First player to reach or exceed 3,000 points.

TURN MECHANICS
  1. Roll all active dice.
  2. Set aside at least one scoring die or valid scoring combination. 
     Every set-aside die must contribute to a valid score.
  3. Choose to bank accumulated points (ending turn) or roll remaining dice.

SCORING COMBINATIONS
  Combination                     Points
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Single 1                        100
  Single 5                        50
  Three 1s                        1,000
  Three-of-a-Kind (2s-6s)          Face Value x 100
  Four-of-a-Kind                  1,000
  Five-of-a-Kind                  2,000
  Six-of-a-Kind                   3,000
  Full Straight (1-2-3-4-5-6)     1,500
  Three Pairs                     750

TURN RULES
  * Bust: Roll zero scoring dice, turn ends immediately and turn points are lost.
  * Hot Dice: Set aside all 6 dice into scoring combinations, re-roll all 6 dice 
    to continue accumulating points in the same turn.
  * Match End: When a player reaches 3,000 points, all other players receive one 
    final turn in the round to attempt to beat the highest score.

7. FREIGHTER.YACHT.DINGHY
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GAME OVERVIEW
  * Players: 2 or more (alternating turns).
  * Dice Pool: 5x d6.
  * Structure: Best of 3 rounds.

TURN MECHANICS
  Each player receives up to 4 rolls per round to unlock cargo sequence:
    1. Freighter: Requires a 6.
    2. Yacht: Requires a 5 (Must be claimed after Freighter).
    3. Dinghy: Requires a 4 (Must be claimed after Yacht).
  (Required dice are set aside automatically as they land).

CARGO SCORING & REROLLS
  * Once all three vessels (6, 5, and 4) are claimed, remaining two dice form Cargo.
  * Combined total of remaining two dice is your round score.
  * If rolls remain within 4-roll allowance, player may re-roll both cargo dice 
    together (both or neither).
  * Failure to Qualify: Failing to claim all three vessels within 4 rolls scores 0.
  * Resolution: Highest cumulative score after 3 rounds wins. Ties trigger 
    sudden-death round.

8. ROYAL UR
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GAME OVERVIEW
  * Players: 2.
  * Dice Pool: 5x d4 (marked points system).
  * Pieces: 3 pieces per player on shared 16-square track.
  * Objective: First player to bring 2 pieces home wins.

MOVEMENT DICE CALCULATION
  Roll 5x d4. Count total upward-facing marked points (0 to 5) for distance.

  Marked Points   Movement Distance   Special Effect
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  0               0 Squares           Triggers Setback Rule
  1 to 5          1 to 5 Squares      Move one piece standard distance

MOVEMENT & BOARD MECHANICS
  * Move a single piece full exact count rolled. Exact count to enter/land home.
  * Cannot land on your own pieces.
  * Capture: Landing on opponent's piece sends it back to start.
  * Rosettes (Squares 4, 8, 12, 16): Landing on a Rosette grants extra roll.
  * Setback Rule: Rolling 0 or having no legal moves forces player to move one of 
    their own pieces backward 4 squares (or to start if occupied).

9. ROLLBOTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GAME OVERVIEW
  * Players: 2.
  * Starting Health: 16 Integrity Points (IP) each (Player 2 starts with 17 IP).
  * Objective: Reduce opponent's IP to zero.

LOADOUT SELECTION
  Each player selects 1 Weapon and 1 Cyberware Ability at match start.

  Weapons:
    * Mono-Blade (d8): Bypasses 50% of target's Firewall.
    * Smart Pistol (d6): Damage doubles if Accuracy roll is 7+; critical 
      damage bypasses 50% of Firewall.
    * Rail Rifle (d12): Bypasses Firewall completely. Requires 1 turn to 
      charge before firing.
    * Sidearm (d4): Default fallback weapon when primary weapon is disabled.

COMBAT CYCLE
  1. Attacker: Rolls Accuracy die (d10) and assigned Weapon Damage die together, 
     confirming attack to resolve.
  2. Defender Choice:
       * Dodge (d6): Roll higher than attacker's Accuracy to negate all damage. 
         Ties or lower take full damage.
       * Firewall (d8): Subtract Firewall roll from incoming damage. Natural 8 
         (Hard Lockdown) negates all damage completely.

10. BONEHEADS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GAME OVERVIEW
  * Players: 2.
  * Board Layout: 3 columns per player (A, B, C), 3 slots each (9 slots total).
  * Objective: Fill all slots while maximizing column scoring multipliers and 
    destroying opponent dice.

TURN MECHANICS
  Roll 1x d6 and place it into any available slot in your columns to end turn.

COLUMN STACKING MULTIPLIERS
  Matching numbers in same column multiply combined value:
    Column Score = (Die Face x Occurrences) x Occurrences
    * Single Die: Face Value (1x)
    * Pair: (Face x 2) x 2
    * Triple: (Face x 3) x 3

SMASHING MECHANIC
  Placing a die in a column destroys all matching dice in opponent's directly 
  opposing column. Remaining opponent dice collapse down to fill gaps.

GAME END
  Game ends immediately when either player fills their 9th slot. Highest score wins.

11. FREE ROLL
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERVIEW
  * Players: 1.
  * Pool Limit: Up to 20 total dice (Selectable mix of d4, d6, d8, d10, d12, d20).
  * Function: Open sandbox mode without scoring, win conditions, or turn limits. 
    Includes optional subtotals toggle per die type.
