ROLLS — THE SPOKEN SCRIPT
Every line of text the app says, where it says it, and what makes it appear.
Taken from the shipped code, not from memory. Plain text, for copy and paste.

===============================================================================
0. THE SHARED CHROME — the same in all eleven games
===============================================================================

THE ROLL BUTTON (the big one at the bottom)
  Its label is the phase of the turn. Every game uses the same four states:

  "Roll"        The dice are on the table and it is your turn to throw.
  "Rolling"     Shown while the dice are in the air. The button is disabled;
                the word is there so the button does not look broken.
  "Pass"        The turn is over and scored. Tapping hands the device on.
  "Done"        The match is over. Tapping opens the result screen.

  Rolls and Free Roll differ, because they are not turn games:
  "Begin"       Rolls only, before the first throw of a turn.
  "Finish"      Rolls only, once every remaining die is selected — confirming
                banks them all and ends the turn.
  "(3 dice selected)"  Under the button in Rolls, once at least one die is
                selected and not all of them are. Singular reads "(1 die
                selected)".

THE HANDOVER GATE (full screen, black over the wood)
  Appears the moment a turn ends in any two-player game.
  Line 1  The turn summary — see each game below. Absent on the first turn.
  Line 2  "Pass to Ada"      — the name of whoever is up next.
  Line 3  Both players' names and scores.
  Button  "I'm Ready"        — tapping it starts the next turn and is what
                               stops the next player seeing the last one's
                               screen.

THE RESULT SCREEN
  Appears when the match is decided.
  Headline  "Ada Wins"   or  "Draw"
  Reason    One line saying why, where the game has one (a Jackpot, a System
            Draw, a target reached).
  Buttons   "Play Again"  and  "Back to Games"

THE TURN-COMPLETE CAPTION (Rolls only)
  Appears over the tray the moment the last die is banked, keyed to the score,
  because Rolls is golf-scored and low is good:
    0        "Perfect"
    1–3      "Excellent"
    4–5      "Great"
    6–10     "Good"
    11–19    (nothing — the band is deliberately silent)
    20+      "You're going to need some luck!"

SETTINGS (the gear, top right of every game)
  "Settings", "Music", "Music Volume", "Sound Effects", "Effects Volume",
  "Table Color", "Done".
  "Saved for Hog. Each game keeps its own."   under the colour picker inside a
                                               game.
  "The same in every game."                   the same line when the music
                                               scope is set to app-wide.
  "Music Scope" (hub only), with the footer changing as it is flipped:
      "Each game remembers its own track and volume."
      "One track and volume across every game."
  "How to Play"              inside a game — opens that game's guide.
  "How to Play — Every Game" on the hub — opens the bubble cluster, one
                             bubble per game, each opening that game's guide.
  "Pick a game to read its rules."  under the cluster's title.

THE HUB
  "Rolls"  — the wordmark under the app's die mark. Each tile shows only the
  game's name under its artwork. If a resource is missing, a banner names the
  file rather than failing silently.

2. BASEBALL DICE
===============================================================================
INTRO (the first screen, before anything else)
  "How long a game?"
  "3 Innings"   with "Short — about 30 at-bats"
  "6 Innings"   with "Medium — about 60 at-bats"
  "9 Innings"   with "Extra long — about 90 at-bats"
  Chosen once. There is no way back to it without starting a new match.

THE STYLE CARDS  (appear at the start of each half-inning, pitcher holding)
  Headline  "Ada — pick your pitching style"
  Cards     "Power Pitcher"  "Mostly fastballs · patient hitters sit on it"
            "Crafty"         "Mostly changeups · contact hitters wait"
            "Breaking Ball"  "Mostly sliders · power hitters guess it"
  Button    "Pick a Style" until one is chosen, then "Hide It" — which covers
            the choice before the device changes hands.
  Warning   "Bo must not see this" — on screen while the style is chosen.

THE SWING CARDS  (appear at every at-bat, batter holding)
  Headline  "Bo — pick your swing"
  Cards     "Patient Swing"  "d8 · sits on the Fastball"
            "Contact Swing"  "d10 · sits on the Changeup"
            "Power Swing"    "d12 · sits on the Slider"
  Button    "Pick a Swing" until one is chosen, then "Swing" — which throws
            both sides' dice together.

READING THE THROW (appears the moment the dice settle)
  Line 1  "Fastball vs Power Swing  −"   the pitch, the swing, and whether the
          matchup was favoured (+), even (=) or unfavoured (−).
  Line 2  "9 − 4 = +5"                   batter's roll, pitcher's roll, gap.
  Line 3  the result, one of:
            "Strikeout."
            "Field out."
            "Double play. Two down."        (field out, runner on first,
                                             pitcher's roll even)
            "Walk."  /  "Walk, forced in."  (the second when it forces a run)
            "Single — 1 in."   the result's name and the runs it drove in;
                               the same shape for a double or a triple
            "Sacrifice fly — a run scores."
            "Caught at the wall."
            "Caught at the wall — third out, the run does not count."
            "Home run!"  /  "Home run — 3 score!"
            "Double max — it is gone!"      (both of a favoured swing's dice
                                             on their maximum: automatic)
  Button  "Next Batter"
  Strip   "1 out · 2 on · 3-2"  — outs, runners and the score, always visible.

9. ROLLBOTS
===============================================================================
INTRO (setup, each runner in turn)
  "Ada — choose your loadout"
  "Weapon"     then the three weapon cards, each with its die and its trick.
  "Cyberware"  then the four ability cards, each with its cost.
  "Choose Above"  → "Lock In"  once both are picked.
  "Begin The Duel"

ATTACKING
  Header    "Ada — your move"        with "Pick a move"
  Buttons   "Attack"  "Charge"  "Swap Loadout"  "Activate Quantum Phase"
  Swapping  "Ada — switch weapon", "Pick a weapon, or cancel",
            "Switching costs your turn, and any held charge.", "Cancel"
  After the throw:
            "Mono-Blade · accuracy 8"
            "Accuracy 8 · damage 6 — send it when you have read them"
            Button "Send It"  — nothing is sent until the attacker has read
            their own roll.

DEFENDING
  Header    "Bo — incoming"
  Line      "Accuracy 8, damage 6 — answer it"
  Cards     "Dodge"     and its d6
            "Firewall"  "Roll a d8 and subtract it from the damage. A natural
                        8 stops everything."
  Button    "Rolling Defence" → "Resolve"  — the defender reads their own roll
            before it is applied, the same as the attacker.

RESOLUTION (one line, after Resolve)
  "Dodged it entirely."
  "The Firewall swallowed it."
  "Hard Lockdown — the whole shot absorbed."
  "Bo took 6."      /  "Critical — Bo took 11."
  "Phased clean through — and hit back for 3."
  "The Overclock left you wide open. You take this one in full."
  Result    "Ada Wins", or "Draw" with "System Draw" as the reason.

11. FREE ROLL
===============================================================================
Intro         An empty table and the dice picker: "d4 d6 d8 d10 d12 d20" with
              + and − on each, and a running "7 / 20".
Button        "Roll"
              "Add some dice first"  — the subtitle when the table is empty.
              "Roll to total"        — with Totals on and nothing thrown yet.
Toggle        "Totals"
Buttons       "Clear"
Readout       "3d6 · 11"  per die type, and the throw's own total, only when
              Totals is on.
There is no handover, no summary and no result screen: Free Roll has no
opponent and nothing to end.
